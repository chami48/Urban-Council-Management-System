# 🏛️ Horana Urban Council Smart Management System

A modern, digital-first platform designed to **transform local government services** into a faster, smarter, and more citizen‑friendly experience. From online payments to service requests, licensing, and municipal finance everything is now just a click away.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Managing urban council services shouldn’t be inconvenient. This system ensures that residents, staff, and administrators experience a smooth and transparent workflow.

With this system, users can:

✨ Submit service requests online  
🧾 Apply for licenses & permits digitally  
💳 Pay taxes, fines & fees securely  
🛠️ Manage municipal finances with accuracy  
📊 View dashboards & generate reports  
👥 Manage staff & user accounts

Whether you're a **citizen**, **officer**, or **administrator**, this platform enhances efficiency and transparency for all.

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 User Roles

| 👤 Role             | Permissions                                                      |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Citizen**         | Submit requests, pay bills, track status, download receipts      |
| **Admin**           | Full system control, approvals, CRUD operations, user management |
| **Finance Officer** | Manage payments, expenses, salaries, finance reports             |
| **License Officer** | Process licenses, renewals, verifications                        |
| **HR Officer**      | Manage employees, attendance, salary bands                       |

---

## 🧩 Core Modules & Features

### 📝 1. **Citizen Service Request Management (CSR)**

* Submit, track, and manage requests (garbage, repairs, complaints)  
* Automated status updates  
* Admin approval flow

### 📄 2. **License & Permit System**

* Apply for business licenses  
* Vehicle permits, trade certificates, construction permits  
* Renewals & digital approvals

### 💰 3. **Finance & Payment Management**

* Online payments for taxes, fines, and fees  
* Manage municipal expenses, vehicles, assets  
* Automatic receipt generation (PDF)  
* Monthly & annual finance reports

### 🧑‍💼 4. **Salary & HR Management**

* Staff profiles, attendance & payroll  
* Salary calculations  
* Leave & allowance management

### 🔐 5. **User & Admin Management**

* Role-based access control  
* Secure login system  
* CRUD operations for all user types
